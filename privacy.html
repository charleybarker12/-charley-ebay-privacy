Charley eBay Listing Bridge Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

This policy covers the dedicated listing tool at charley-ebay-bridge.charleybarker12.chatgpt.site. The tool is operated by Charley Barker for the seller's own eBay listings.

Information and use

With the seller's authorization, the tool accesses the eBay account identifier, active listings, and listing details to review and make requested changes. It stores the account identifier and an encrypted OAuth refresh token so the seller does not need to authorize every session. Listing information is processed only for the requested work. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

The tool currently does not persist listing contents or photos. The connection is retained until the seller disconnects, revokes access through eBay, or the token expires. A verified eBay marketplace account deletion notification removes the matching stored connection. The seller can also use the tool's disconnect control.

Service providers and contact

ChatGPT Sites hosts the tool and processes technical data necessary to serve it. eBay supplies authorized account and listing data. Questions about these practices may be sent to charley@barkeritsolutions.com.
